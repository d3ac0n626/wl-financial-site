Common Questions

Everything You Need to Know

Straight answers — no fluff.

Do I need a degree or prior experience?

Not at all. We provide every resource — from licensing prep to sales scripts — so you can start from zero and succeed.

How soon can I start earning?

Most agents close their first sale within 2–4 weeks of completing training. Your income accelerates as you gain confidence and build a pipeline.

Is this a solo gig or a team environment?

Both. You build your own book of business, but you're never alone. Our team is on daily calls, group chats, and 1-on-1 coaching sessions.

What kind of products do I sell?

Life insurance, annuities, and other financial products that genuinely help families protect their futures. This is meaningful work.

Can I really work from anywhere?

Yes. 100%. Our agents work from home, coffee shops, co-working spaces — wherever they have a laptop and internet.

What's the catch?

There isn't one — but this isn't a get-rich-quick scheme either. Success takes effort, consistency, and coachability. We bring the system; you bring the work ethic.
